## Root-cause evidence

Read-only inspection of the applied function bodies and trigger order shows:

- `transition_daily_log_status` correctly returns the summary to its owner and creates a revision assignment.
- The final `zz_preserve_daily_log_summary_approval_route` trigger preserves the summary's `submitted_to_permission='approve'`, including while rejected. A direct SQL read confirmed `rejected/approve`; the earlier expectation of `edit` was a diagnostic harness assumption, corrected rather than treated as a product regression.
- `submit_daily_log_summary_legacy_v1` unconditionally updates `submitted_to_permission='approve'` while the row is still rejected, before calling the canonical submit transition.
- On that nominally redundant update, the earlier `daily_log_step_touch` trigger temporarily changes the rejected row's route to `edit`. `enforce_daily_log_room_status` observes a rejected-state routing change and requires the old route's `approve` permission from the summarizer. It raises before the final route-preservation trigger or the actual submit transition.

Thus the sender is blocked by an intermediate rejected-state update, not missing CHT permissions, missing source data, or a failed publication receipt. Granting approval to the summarizer would conceal the bug and violate role separation; do not do it.
